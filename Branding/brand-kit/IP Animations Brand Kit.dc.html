CONSTRUCTION

Space, size, don'ts

The logo is never crowded, never tiny, never altered.

Clear space
IPanimations

A margin equal to the height of the mark on all sides. Nothing enters it.

Minimum size
IPanimations
120 PX · 30 MM

Below 120px use mono. Below 80px use the icon only.

Don't
  • Colour the mark or “IP”.
  • Recolour letters or change the sequence.
  • Flatten the wave or set it on one line.
  • Swap the typeface; add outlines, shadows or gradients.
  • Place the colour version on photos or busy backgrounds.
  • Put the colour version on Grape or Sunbeam.

MOTION

Animated logo

An Ink ball bounces along the tops of the letters, never touching them; each letter squashes as it is passed. Two variants, used interchangeably. The last frame of either loop is the static logo.

Timing
380ms per letter · 3.8s per pass. Wave loops each pass; Switch resolves every two.
Where
Stings, end cards (two passes, hold). Website hero on load (one pass). Loaders. Never in documents or email.
A · Wave — the ball rides the wave
B · Switch — each landing turns a letter off; the next pass turns it back on

04 · COLOURIP ANIMATIONS · BRAND KIT
PALETTE

Two and two

Two backgrounds, two brand colours, neutral text. Six further colours live only inside the logo. That restraint is what lets the brand be colourful without being childish.

Contrast
Body text is only Ink on Paper or Paper on Charcoal. Grape may be text at 15px+ on Paper. Sunbeam is never text — it sits behind Ink text as a highlight, or is a dot, a line, a surface.

06 · RULESIP ANIMATIONS · BRAND KIT
TEN RULES

Ten rules

If something looks childish, one of these has been broken.

01
One Grape thing per screen

A button, a link, a panel or the dot. Not two.

06
The dot ends headlines

0.2em round; Sunbeam on Charcoal, Grape on Paper. It replaces the full stop.

02
One or two Sunbeam things

A highlighted word, a marker dot, an underline. Occasionally a whole cover.

07
Highlights are flat

Sunbeam behind Ink text, 3px radius, .14em side padding. One or two words, never a line.

03
Text is neutral

Ink on Paper, Paper on Charcoal. Colour goes behind, beside or after a word — not into it.

08
Soft corners, not pills

Buttons 6–8px, cards 14px, tiles 22.5%. Round only the ball and the dots.

04
Small type, big space

Headlines never exceed H1. At least 60% of any screen is background.

09
The logo is the only rainbow

Bubblegum, Mint, Sky, Tangerine never appear outside the logo. No gradients.

05
Three rows

Top bar · content · footer. Content is centred or anchored to one edge, never spread to four corners.

10
Motion is for the logo and the work

Interface motion is 200ms ease-out fades. Squash-and-stretch belongs to the logo and the films.
